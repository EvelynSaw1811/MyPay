## Core Pitch In One Sentence

MyPay is a mobile-first, multi-currency e-wallet and group expense settlement platform that combines wallets, shared collections, flexible split rules, currency conversion, net settlement, notifications, and reporting on top of a scalable Spring Cloud microservice architecture.

## Main Investor Message

MyPay should be presented as more than a payment app. The strongest positioning is:

- A social finance platform for people who repeatedly share expenses.
- A wallet system that supports multiple currencies and settlement flows.
- A backend architecture ready for modular growth, operational scaling, and future fintech integrations.
- A product that reduces the friction between "we spent money together" and "everyone is settled correctly".

## Suggested Timing

- 2 min: Problem, story, and market
- 4 min: Product walkthrough

- 5 min: System design, tech stack, and engineering strength
- 3 min: UI and user experience
