# 01 - Opening Investor Story

## Goal Of This Section

Start with a real pain point investors can understand quickly: group spending is common, but settlement is still fragmented, manual, and uncomfortable.

## Suggested Opening

"When friends travel, housemates share bills, teams collect money, or families split purchases, the payment itself is usually easy. The hard part is remembering who paid, who owes whom, whether tax or service charge was included, which currency was used, and how to settle without confusion. MyPay solves that full journey in one platform."

## Problem

Current users often need several tools:

- One app to chat.
- One app to calculate split amounts.
- One app to transfer money.
- A spreadsheet or notes app to track who has paid.
- Manual reminders for unsettled balances.

This creates friction, delayed repayment, social discomfort, and unreliable records.

## MyPay's Answer

MyPay combines:

- Multi-currency wallets.
- Group collections.
- Expense splitting.
- Settlement and netting.
- Currency conversion.
- Notifications.
- Reports and ledger views.

The product is designed to close the loop from expense creation to final settlement.

## Investor Framing

The attractive angle is not only "payments". It is "shared financial coordination". MyPay targets recurring situations where people need clarity, trust, and convenient settlement across groups.

## Slide Talking Points

- Money movement is easy, but money coordination is still painful.
- MyPay owns the workflow around shared expenses.
- The system is built with microservices, event messaging, and modular fintech-ready components.
- The platform can expand into merchant bill splitting, travel groups, family finance, student communities, and SME team expenses.
