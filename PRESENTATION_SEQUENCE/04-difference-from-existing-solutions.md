# 04 - Difference From Existing Solutions

## Existing Solution Types

### Traditional E-Wallets

Examples: Touch 'n Go eWallet, GrabPay, Boost, PayPal, Wise.

Strengths:

- Payment and transfer rails.
- User wallet balances.
- Merchant payment support.
- Some rewards or financial services.

Gaps MyPay targets:

- They do not usually manage full group expense workflows.
- They do not deeply model who paid, who owes, tax split, exact split, and collection-level settlement.
- They often stop after payment, while MyPay tracks the social debt before and after payment.

### Expense Split Apps

Examples: Splitwise, Settle Up, Tricount.

Strengths:

- Good group expense tracking.
- Simple debt visibility.
- Useful for trips and recurring shared expenses.

Gaps MyPay targets:

- Many do not include an integrated wallet model.
- Payment can remain external.
- Multi-currency settlement and wallet balance integration may be limited or indirect.
- Backend architecture is not visible to users, but MyPay is built as a modular fintech platform.

### Bank Transfer And Manual Tracking

Examples: mobile banking plus spreadsheet or chat.

Strengths:

- Familiar.
- Direct bank movement.

Gaps MyPay targets:

- Manual calculation.
- No shared ledger.
- No split rule engine.
- No role-based collection management.
- No automated notifications.
- No netting.

## MyPay Differentiation

MyPay combines the best parts of wallet apps and split apps:

- Wallet balances plus group expense logic.
- Flexible split strategies, not only equal split.
- Netting to reduce unnecessary transfers.
- Multi-currency conversion path.
- Notifications from real events.
- Reporting and ledger views.
- Microservice architecture designed for scale and independent service evolution.

## Investor-Safe Differentiation Statement

"MyPay does not compete only as another wallet. It competes as a settlement layer for shared financial relationships. Existing wallets move money; existing split apps calculate obligations. MyPay connects obligation, wallet, settlement, and reporting in one flow."
