# 02 - Market And Target Users

## Market

MyPay sits at the intersection of:

- Digital wallets.
- Peer-to-peer payments.
- Group expense management.
- Multi-currency personal finance.
- Social finance and shared spending.

## Target Users

### Primary Users

- Students sharing rent, meals, supplies, and trips.
- Young adults and friend groups splitting activities.
- Travelers managing expenses in multiple currencies.
- Housemates managing recurring bills.
- Families tracking shared spending.

### Secondary Users

- Small teams managing informal reimbursements.
- Event organizers collecting and settling shared costs.
- Clubs, societies, and communities.
- Cross-border friend groups or users who hold different currencies.

## User Pain Points

- "I forgot who paid."
- "I do not know how much I owe after tax."
- "We used different currencies."
- "One person paid for many things and nobody tracked it."
- "Everyone transferred separately, creating too many small payments."
- "There is no simple ledger after the event."

## Why The Timing Is Attractive

- Users are already comfortable with e-wallets and mobile banking.
- Social and group-based spending is frequent.
- Cross-border travel and online purchases make currency handling more common.
- Users expect real-time updates, clean mobile UX, and digital records.

## Presentation Angle

Show investors that MyPay is not trying to replace every bank or wallet immediately. It starts with a focused, high-friction use case: group money coordination. From there, it can grow into deeper wallet features, partner integrations, merchant payments, subscription management, and financial insights.
